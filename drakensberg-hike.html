Drakensberg hiking escape

A two-day guided hiking experience in South Africa's dramatic Drakensberg mountains.

About this adventure

Explore mountain paths with a local guide, take in the highland scenery and enjoy time outdoors at a relaxed group pace. The exact route depends on weather, trail conditions and the group's experience.

Example itinerary

  1. Day one: Meet the guide, check equipment and begin the first guided trail.
  2. Day two: Continue exploring the area, then return to the agreed meeting point.

Trip details

  • Duration: 2 days
  • Location: Drakensberg, South Africa
  • Bring suitable walking shoes, weather-appropriate clothing, water and personal medication.
  • Ask the organiser about transport, accommodation, meals and required fitness before booking.

Example fee: R 2,800 per person. See fee details and inclusions.
