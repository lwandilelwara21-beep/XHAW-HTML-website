About Adventure Escape SA

We help people experience South Africa through locally guided outdoor adventures.

Our approach

We work with local guides who know the routes, communities and environments they share with visitors. Our trips are planned in small groups so there is time to learn, explore and travel responsibly.

What matters to us

  • Local knowledge and experienced guides
  • Respect for South African communities and nature
  • Clear trip information before you travel
  • Memorable experiences at a comfortable pace
